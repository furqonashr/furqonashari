The challenge

A baby-care brand selling on Shopee, with two paid channels working side by side: Meta Collaborative Ads (CPAS) driving traffic into the shop, and Shopee Ads inside the marketplace. The goal was more sales from the same budget, not a bigger budget.

What moved, Jul to Oct 2026

Shopee Ads ROAS

Sales from ads / ad spend, Shopee Seller Centre

Meta CPAS ROAS doubled (6.4x to 12.9x) and Shopee Ads ROAS rose 60% (14.7x to 23.4x). A new GMV Max product group reached 28.3x in its first month, and the brand did Rp71.9M in store sales in a single day on the 11.11 campaign.

How I optimize

  1. Find the leaks firstChecked placements and ad-set overlap before adding spend: a placement that kept absorbing retargeting budget at a very low ROAS, and several retargeting ad sets bidding against each other for the same audience.
  2. Refresh the hero creative before it burns outThe best-selling creative carried about a third of sales but was shown too often (frequency near 4), so new versions were lined up behind it.
  3. Test angles, then scale the winnerEmotional, mother-focused messages beat milestone-style numbers in testing, so budget moved to the angle with the best ROAS and low frequency; creatives with high CTR but no sales were reworked.
  4. Add GMV Max for the best sellersA new GMV Max product group for the strongest products reached 28.3x ROAS in its first month.
