From suspended to top 3 on Google Maps

Rebuilding and optimizing a dental clinic's Google Business Profile, from a suspended, duplicated listing to top-3 rankings across a 4 km radius.

The challenge

A dental clinic whose Google Business Profile was suspended and flagged as a duplicate in January 2025, so it did not appear when people searched for a dentist nearby.

The only other marketing was Instagram. That made Google Maps the one channel that could bring in new patients from search, and made the results easy to trace back to the profile.

How I work

  1. Audit firstScore the profile against a checklist (business info, photos, reviews, categories, posts, products and services) and against the local competition, so month one starts from facts.
  2. Propose changesA prioritized list goes to the client for approval before anything on the profile is touched.
  3. Keyword researchTen keywords picked from how patients actually search, such as the generic dentist and clinic terms, near-me searches and individual treatments, each tied to a service and an area.
  4. OptimizeBusiness information, services and products, gallery, reviews and posts, kept consistent with the chosen keywords.
  5. Track in a growing radiusRankings are tracked with Local Falcon, starting at 1 km. If a keyword does well, the radius grows the next month (2 km, 3 km and so on). If it slips, the radius stays put until it recovers.
  6. Close the loop with patientsReception asks every new patient how they found the clinic, so Google-sourced patients can be counted.
