About

Data first, then the creative.

I'm Furqon Ashari, a Performance Marketing Specialist based in Jakarta. Most of my days go into planning, running, and fine-tuning paid campaigns on Meta, Google, and Shopee Ads for brands in e-commerce, healthcare, and the marketing industry.

I like to start with the numbers: where the money actually goes, who's really buying, and which ad makes people stop scrolling. Once that picture is clear, the right move usually is too. That's how a baby-care brand I handle doubled its Meta CPAS ROAS in four months, and how a healthcare client got more conversions while spending 40% less.

Before ads, I spent a year in local SEO and content writing. It taught me two things I still use every day: small details decide whether people find a business, and good copy is written for people, not algorithms.
